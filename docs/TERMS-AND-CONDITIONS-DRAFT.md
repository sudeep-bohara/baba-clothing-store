# Terms and Conditions — draft

**DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR PRODUCTION USE**

These are the plain-language terms of the concept site, plus the requirements a real store's
terms must satisfy. They are not terms of sale and they are not legal advice.

## 1. Status of this site

An unofficial concept study prepared to demonstrate a possible storefront design for Baba
Clothing. It is not operated by, endorsed by or affiliated with the brand unless the owner says
so in writing. Nothing on the site is an offer to sell and no contract can be formed by using
it.

## 2. Content accuracy

Product names, descriptions, colours, sizes and prices are sample content. Prices are shown in
rupees for illustration only and reflect no real pricing, stock or promotion. The bag and its
totals demonstrate interaction design; the checkout action states that ordering is not enabled
and no order is created.

## 3. Brand and intellectual property

The Baba Clothing name, the slogan "Your style, Your identity." and any brand marks belong to
their owner. The concept uses a text wordmark and does not recreate the brand's monogram.
Demonstration photographs are CC0 or Public Domain Mark works, recorded with source and creator
in [ASSETS.md](ASSETS.md); they do not depict Baba Clothing products or campaigns. All
demonstration photography must be replaced with owner-approved assets before any public use.

## 4. Permitted use

Browsing, resizing, and testing the interactions — including with a keyboard or a screen reader —
is welcome. The concept must not be presented as the brand's live store, its demonstration
photography must not be passed off as the brand's own, and it must not be used to take orders or
payments from anyone.

## 5. Availability

The concept is provided as it is, for review and discussion. It may change or disappear without
notice, and no warranty is given about its accuracy, availability or fitness for a purpose.
Because no transaction can occur, there is nothing to limit in respect of orders, delivery,
payment or returns.

## 6. Production terms requirements

A real store needs terms defining: website usage; product information; pricing; order
acceptance; payment obligations; customer responsibilities; intellectual property; liability;
applicable law; dispute resolution; and contact details. Each of the following needs its own
approved policy, written from verified operating facts:

| Policy | Must state |
|---|---|
| Shipping and delivery | Locations, methods, charges, delivery estimates, dispatch times, tracking, failed and undeliverable orders |
| Returns and refunds | Eligible products, return window, condition, exchanges, refund method and timing, damaged or incorrect items, statutory exceptions |
| Cancellation | When an order can be cancelled, and what happens after payment or dispatch |
| Payment | Accepted methods, confirmation, failed transactions, refunds |
| Product information | Fabric, sizing, colour, fit, care, imagery, price, availability — only where known |

Publishing a policy page does not by itself create legal compliance. The brand owner must
approve every policy and a qualified professional must review it for the applicable law.

## 7. Contact

The only verified channel is Instagram, `@babaclothing.np`. Address, email, phone and hours are
deliberately withheld until the owner confirms them.
