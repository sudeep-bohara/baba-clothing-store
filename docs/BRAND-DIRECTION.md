# Brand Direction — Baba Clothing

- Visual identity appears predominantly black and white.
- A BA-style monogram is visible.
- Content includes clothing and model/lifestyle presentation.

These are visual observations, not a complete official brand guide. Confirm logo files, exact colors, typography, and usage rules with the owner before production.

## Creative direction
Create a premium, contemporary streetwear/lifestyle storefront with an editorial fashion sensibility. It should feel confident and product-led rather than like a generic marketplace template.

### Visual principles
- Monochrome foundation: near-black, white, restrained neutral greys.
- Strong typographic hierarchy and generous spacing.
- Large, intentional campaign imagery.
- Clean product cards with consistent image ratios.
- Restrained motion; respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Prioritize small-screen usability and fast product discovery.

### Avoid
- Blue as a dominant brand color.
- Excessive gradients, glass effects, or decorative UI.
- Generic stock-photo overload.
- Fake urgency, fake reviews, fake sale prices, or unsupported claims.
- Recreating the BA monogram as an official vector logo. Use a placeholder wordmark until an approved asset is supplied.

## Copy direction
Use “Your style, Your identity.” exactly where appropriate. Additional brand story and product claims must be marked as placeholder or omitted until verified.

## Asset handling
Prefer owner-provided product and campaign photographs. Until then, use properly licensed temporary demo imagery and make replacement straightforward. Never imply placeholder images depict actual inventory.
